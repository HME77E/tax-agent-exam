NEBOSH GIC1 – Practice with Model Answers

Independent NEBOSH-style training resource. These are original practice questions, not official NEBOSH examination questions. Each task carries 10 training marks. Answer in English and link points to the scenario.

Mock Paper 1: Construction Site – Tower Project

Several scaffold platforms lack toe boards, and workers say they have not received site induction. The supervisor tells workers to continue because the client is visiting. A worker reports a near miss but no record is made. The safety policy was signed three years ago, and management reviews focus only on cost and schedule.
